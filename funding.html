TRANSPARENCY IN RADICAL ORGANIZING

Funding of Communist Groups in the United States

Most U.S. communist and Marxist-Leninist organizations are small and rely primarily on member dues and individual donations. Financial transparency is limited. One notable exception involves significant external funding through networks associated with Neville Roy Singham.

KEY INSIGHT
Most groups are still small and dues-funded
CPUSA, WWP, FRSO and others rely primarily on members paying $2–10/month.
KEY INSIGHT
One network changed the game
Neville Roy Singham’s funding (hundreds of millions routed through nonprofits) has dramatically scaled the PSL ecosystem and aligned organizing.

Funding Patterns and Transparency

Standard Model (Most Groups)
  • Monthly or annual membership dues (often sliding scale; e.g. CPUSA $10/mo standard, $2 for low-income — explicitly not tax-deductible)
  • Individual donations via website forms
  • Fund drives for newspapers (People’s World for CPUSA; Workers World for WWP)
  • Merchandise, events, local branch fundraising, and "sustainer" recurring programs
  • Very limited public disclosure requirements as they are primarily political organizations rather than large 501(c)(3)s

CPUSA states: “The work of the Communist Party is funded solely by donations from grassroots supporters and allies... We receive no grants or corporate sponsorship.”

Major External Funding Exception: Singham Network

The PSL and affiliated organizations (People’s Forum, ANSWER Coalition) receive substantial support from tech billionaire Neville Roy Singham (former Thoughtworks chairman, now Shanghai-based).

  • People’s Forum: ~$20–22 million (2017–2023) via donor-advised funds (Goldman Sachs Philanthropy Fund and shells). Specific transfers include $2.5M (2017), $4.4M (2018), $12.43M (2019+)
  • CodePink (Singham wife Jodie Evans co-founder): ~$1.4M (roughly 25% of donations)
  • Broader network (People’s Forum, Justice & Education Fund, BreakThrough News, Tricontinental, ANSWER): Reports of $278 million+ since 2017
  • PSL leadership heavily overlaps with funded entities (e.g. Claudia De la Cruz, Manolo De Los Santos)

Singham has publicly been called a “Marxist comrade” by the People’s Forum (2021 X post).

Singham Network: Detailed Funding Flows & Structure

Mechanism: Funds routed through U.S. donor-advised funds (e.g. Goldman Sachs Philanthropy Fund, United Community Fund) and entities like Justice & Education Fund (JEF) and People’s Support Foundation to obscure direct transfers. Many recipient orgs use UPS store / mailbox addresses in filings.

Key Amounts & Timeline (public reports):

  • 2017–2022: Over $20.4 million to The People’s Forum alone (Singham + Evans via shells)
  • Broader network: ~$278 million across six primary nonprofits (2017–2023 per multiple investigations)
  • JEF: Major grantmaker receiving tens of millions from Singham entities
  • International flows: Millions directed to foreign radical and media groups (e.g. Indian outlets, Tricontinental)

Overlaps with PSL: The People’s Forum serves as a hub. PSL staff/leadership run or co-direct funded projects. “Liberation Centers” and protest infrastructure benefit. ANSWER Coalition (WWP/PSL linked) organizes many actions with Singham-backed support.

Alleged Influence: Singham maintains close ties to Chinese state media in Shanghai. Groups have amplified pro-CCP narratives on Xinjiang, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and “no war on China” campaigns while criticizing U.S. policy.

Congressional & Official Scrutiny (2025–2026)

  • House Ways & Means Committee (Chairman Jason Smith): Multiple letters demanding records from People’s Forum, BreakThrough News, Tricontinental on foreign funding, fiscal sponsorships, and Singham ties (as of May 2026 deadlines).
  • Senate Judiciary Committee (Sen. Chuck Grassley): Requests for DOJ/FBI investigation into People’s Forum and CodePink for potential FARA (Foreign Agents Registration Act) violations due to Chinese government influence via Singham.
  • Concerns center on tax-exempt status being used to channel foreign-linked funds into U.S. political activism and propaganda.

Sources: Official committee letters, tax filings analyzed by reporters.

Additional Smaller or Niche Groups

Beyond the primary organizations above, several smaller Marxist-Leninist or communist formations exist, primarily funded through the same dues + donations model with very limited public financial data:

  • Freedom Road Socialist Organization (FRSO) and Revolutionary Communists of America (RCA): Member dues, sustainers, events. Focus on cadre building and movement work.
  • American Party of Labor (APL) and Party of Communists USA (PCUSA): Anti-revisionist or traditional ML; dues and small donations. International affiliations noted but no large external U.S. funding reported.
  • Local collectives and historical remnants (e.g. various Red Guards successors or Trotskyist-influenced groups) operate at very small scale.

Note: A 2024 splinter called the American Communist Party (associated with Haz Al-Din and Jackson Hinkle) emerged with a populist/"MAGA Communism" orientation but is not a traditional Marxist-Leninist organization.
