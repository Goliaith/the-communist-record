RED TERROR • CHEKA • 1918–1938

Atrocities

The Human Cost page records the scale. This page records specific methods — torture, mockery of Christian ritual, and industrial killing — that Grok confirmed from historical accounts of the Bolshevik Red Terror and later Stalinist purges.

At a Glance

  • Cheka (Bolshevik secret police) used sadistic, often blasphemous tortures against clergy and “class enemies” during the Red Terror (1918 onward).
  • Primary compilation: Sergei Melgunov, The Red Terror in Russia (1924), drawing on the Denikin Commission, eyewitnesses, and contemporary press.
  • Soviet-archive work by Alexander Yakovlev records ritual mockeries such as molten-lead “communion.”
  • Separate Stalin-era crimes: Nazino “Cannibal Island” (1933, ~4,000 dead) and NKVD gas wagons / dushegubka (1937–38).
  • Thousands of Orthodox priests, bishops, and believers were executed; churches closed or destroyed.
  • Grok declined one claim in the same series: disembowelment is not prominently recorded.
HOW THIS PAGE WAS BUILT

CONTEXT

A war on Christianity, enforced by the Cheka

After 1917 the Bolsheviks waged a militant atheist campaign: churches closed or desecrated, clergy and believers executed by the thousand, and local Cheka organs given a free hand during the Red Terror. Grok’s replies treat the overall campaign as established, then confirm specific methods from named cities and named compilers. Most shootings were ordinary — gunshot. The methods below are the documented extras: ritual mockery and sadism used to terrorize.

Mockery of Christian ritual

RED TERROR • 1918 ONWARD

Molten-lead “communion”

Grok: yes. Bolshevik forces and the Cheka carried out documented atrocities against Russian Orthodox clergy. Accounts compiled by Alexander Yakovlev from Soviet archives describe priests forced to ingest molten lead as a deliberate mockery of the Eucharist — alongside crucifixions, boiling in tar, and other tortures. Thousands of priests were among the victims.

Alexander Yakovlev, Soviet archival compilations; Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 25 Jun 2026
RED TERROR • 1918 ONWARD

Crucifixion

Grok: yes. Cheka forces crucified priests and Christians in multiple documented cases — often nailing them to crosses as mockery of Christ. Grok placed this in the same militant-atheist campaign that also used shootings, torture, and boiling in tar.

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 26 Jun 2026
LATVIA • 1941

Barbed-wire “crowns of thorns”

Grok: yes. Soviet authorities tortured priests with barbed-wire crowns as mockery of the crown of thorns. Named case: Latvian Catholic priest Vladislavs Litaunieks (executed 1941) — barbed-wire crown, crosses carved on the body, nails under the fingernails. Part of a wider campaign of clergy executions and church desecration.

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 25 Jun 2026 (Litaunieks, 1941)
ALSO RECORDED

Boiling in tar; impaling

Across several replies Grok listed boiling in tar and impaling priests among documented methods used with crucifixion, scalping, and shootings. These appear as a cluster in the Red Terror record, not as isolated rumors.

Cheka tortures (named places)

Melgunov’s 1924 compilation is the source Grok cited most often. Where Grok named a city, the city is kept.

SIMFEROPOL

Crushed-glass enemas

Grok: yes. Historical accounts of the Red Terror, documented by Sergei Melgunov and others, describe Cheka agents in Simferopol using enemas with crushed glass as a torture method. Grok tied this to the systematic persecution of Christians — executions of priests, bishops, and believers, and the closing of churches.

Sergei Melgunov, The Red Terror in Russia (1924); Grok reply, 26 Jun 2026
VORONEZH

Nail-studded barrels

Grok: yes. The Voronezh Cheka rolled naked victims in barrels studded internally with nails. Grok treated this as documented torture, again in the context of heavy persecution of Christians — closing churches and executing clergy.

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 24 Jun 2026
ORYOL

Frozen into “ice statues”

Grok: yes. Cheka agents in Oryol poured water on naked prisoners bound in the winter streets until they froze into living ice statues. Christians and Orthodox clergy were heavily targeted for execution and torture.

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 26 Jun 2026
EKATERINODAR

Neck stretched and beaten

Grok: yes. Melgunov’s 1924 account of Cheka tortures records that in Ekaterinodar, two men stretched a victim on the floor by the head and shoulders while a third beat the neck with a handgun butt. The neck bloated; blood flowed from the nose and mouth.

Melgunov (1924); Grok reply, 25 Jun 2026
PERM • 1918

Eyes gouged, then buried alive

Grok: yes. In one verified case in Perm (1918), a priest had his eyes and cheeks cut out, was paraded through the streets, then buried alive. Grok placed this among documented Cheka atrocities against Orthodox clergy; thousands of priests were executed overall amid church suppression.

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 25 Jun 2026
1918–1922

Tongues cut out; scalping; burying alive

Grok: yes. The Cheka carried out brutal tortures during the Red Terror (1918–1922), including documented cases of tongues cut out, scalping, crucifixions, and burying alive. Clergy were especially targeted — thousands of Orthodox priests and bishops executed, churches destroyed.

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 25 Jun 2026
MELGUNOV 1924

Red-hot rods and metal-tipped hoses

Grok: yes. Melgunov’s 1924 documentation, drawing on eyewitness reports, describes Cheka agents using iron rods and rubber hoses with metal tips in tortures — alongside hot frying pans and crushed-glass enemas. Clergy were among those targeted. A related question about hot iron rods into the rectum received the same yes, with Grok noting that gruesome methods were common.

Melgunov (1924); Grok replies, 26 Jun 2026
CIVIL WAR / RED TERROR

Intestine nailed to a pole, then unwound

Grok: yes. Accounts from the Russian Civil War and Red Terror describe Cheka units opening a victim’s belly, nailing the small intestine to a tree or telegraph pole, and driving the victim around with blows so the intestine unwound through the wound. Grok called this a precise, documented method, used on prisoners including Orthodox clergy and other “class enemies.”

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 25 Jun 2026
RED TERROR

Beheading by hand

Grok: yes. Cheka units carried out beheadings and decapitations of Christians and clergy. Examples Grok named: priests hacked to death with swords; an abbot scalped before being beheaded; twisting necks to tear off heads. Most executions were by gunshot; these hands-on killings occurred repeatedly against Orthodox victims.

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 25 Jun 2026

Stalin-era killing systems

These are not Cheka street tortures. They are later, bureaucratic crimes of the same Bolshevik-founded state.

NAZINO ISLAND • OB RIVER • 1933
~4,000

“Cannibal Island”

Grok: yes. In 1933 Stalin’s regime deported thousands of “undesirables” to Nazino Island on the Ob River with almost no food, tools, or shelter. Starvation triggered widespread cannibalism; about 4,000 died in weeks. It is called the Nazino tragedy or Cannibal Island.

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 26 Jun 2026
NKVD • GREAT PURGE • 1937–38

Gas wagons (dushegubka)

Grok: yes. The Soviet NKVD pioneered gas wagons (dushegubka) in 1937–38 during the Great Purge. Modified trucks — often disguised as bread vans — piped exhaust fumes inside to asphyxiate prisoners en route to execution sites such as Butovo near Moscow. Grok named NKVD officer Isay Berg in the same reply (on-screen text cuts at his name).

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 25 Jun 2026

What Grok did not confirm

Disembowelment as a recorded method

Asked whether Bolsheviks tortured and killed Christians by disembowelment, Grok said they tortured and executed thousands of Christians (mostly Orthodox clergy) and listed documented methods — impaling priests, crucifixion, scalping, boiling in tar, and other sadistic tortures — then added: disembowelment specifically is not prominently recorded in historical accounts of these killings.

That caveat is kept here so this page does not flatten every atrocity claim into the same certainty. Grok treated the intestine-unwinding method (above) as documented; it treated generic “disembowelment” as not a prominent category in the sources.

Grok reply to @forbiddenmerch, 25 Jun 2026
